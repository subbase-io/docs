## Overview

**Concrete Commitments** are specialized agreements for managing concrete pours on construction projects. Unlike standard material commitments, concrete commitments include:

- Mix design specifications
- Pour scheduling and coordination
- Delivery location management
- Vendor communication for just-in-time delivery

---

## Key Concepts

### Pour

A **Pour** is a scheduled concrete placement event. Each pour specifies:

- **Location** – Where on the job site
- **Mix Design** – The concrete specification
- **Volume** – Cubic yards required
- **Date/Time** – When the pour is scheduled

### Mix Design

The specification for the concrete, including:

- Strength (PSI)
- Slump
- Aggregate size
- Admixtures
- Special requirements (fiber, color, etc.)

## Creating a Concrete Commitment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Commitments">
    Go to **Commitments** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Commitment">
    Click **+ New Commitment** and select **Concrete Commitment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Project">
    Choose the project for this commitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Vendor">
    Choose your concrete supplier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Agreement Details">
    Add reference information and terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Adding Mix Designs

Define the mix designs available under this commitment:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Mix">
    Click **Add Mix Design** to create a new specification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Mix Details">
    - **Name** – Descriptive name (e.g., "4000 PSI Slab Mix")
    - **Strength** – PSI rating
    - **Slump** – Consistency specification
    - **Price per CY** – Committed price per cubic yard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Specifications">
    Include any special requirements or admixtures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The mix is added to your commitment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat for all mix designs in the agreement.

## Scheduling Pours

### Creating a Pour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Pour Schedule">
    Navigate to the **Pours** or **Pour Schedule** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Pour">
    Click **+ Schedule Pour** to create a new pour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Commitment">
    Choose the concrete commitment to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Mix Design">
    Choose the appropriate mix for this pour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Pour Details">
    - **Location** – Where on site (e.g., "Building A, Level 2 Slab")
    - **Volume** – Estimated cubic yards
    - **Date** – Scheduled pour date
    - **Start Time** – When trucks should arrive
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The pour is added to the schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pour Calendar

View all scheduled pours in calendar format:

- **Daily View** – See all pours for a specific day
- **Weekly View** – Plan the week's concrete work
- **Monthly View** – Long-term pour planning

## Pour States

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Scheduled** | Pour planned and on calendar |
| **Confirmed** | Vendor has confirmed the date |
| **In Progress** | Pour is happening |
| **Completed** | Pour finished successfully |
| **Cancelled** | Pour cancelled |
| **Rescheduled** | Moved to a new date |

## Coordinating with Vendors

### Sending Pour Requests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Pour">
    Open the scheduled pour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Request Confirmation">
    Send a request to the vendor to confirm the pour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include Details">
    Add any special instructions or requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The vendor receives notification of the scheduled pour.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Vendor Confirmation

When the vendor confirms:

1. Pour status updates to **Confirmed**
2. You receive notification of confirmation
3. Any changes from vendor are noted

## Day-of-Pour Management

### Pre-Pour Checklist

Before the pour begins:

<Check>Confirm vendor has the pour on schedule</Check>
<Check>Verify mix design is correct</Check>
<Check>Ensure site is ready to receive concrete</Check>
<Check>Confirm equipment and crew are in place</Check>

### During the Pour

Track actual delivery:

1. Record truck arrivals and ticket numbers
2. Note actual volumes delivered
3. Track any issues or delays
4. Capture delivery ticket photos

### Logging Mix Data

Use the **Log Table** to record mix data during and after the pour:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Log Table">
    Navigate to the pour and open the logging view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Fields Inline">
    Click on any editable field (pour date, mix code, location, quantities) to edit directly in the table. Changes are validated in real-time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Variance">
    As you enter actual quantities, the variance column updates automatically to show differences from planned amounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save** to persist your edits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The Log Table supports inline editing for faster data entry. Fields are validated as you type, and variance calculations update immediately.
</Tip>

### Completing the Pour

After the pour:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Actual Volume">
    Record the total cubic yards actually placed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Tickets">
    Attach all delivery tickets from the vendor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note Any Issues">
    Document any problems (delays, quality issues, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark Complete">
    Set the pour status to **Completed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Delivery Tickets

Delivery tickets from concrete vendors are essential documentation for reconciliation and invoicing.

### Adding Tickets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pour">
    Navigate to the pour where you want to add tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Ticket">
    Click **Add Ticket** to open the ticket form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Ticket Details">
    Fill in the ticket number, quantities, and other relevant information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach Files">
    Upload photos or scans of the physical delivery ticket. Multiple file attachments are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit** to save the ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Viewing Ticket List

All tickets for a pour are displayed in the **Ticket List**:

- View ticket numbers, quantities, and status at a glance
- Click any ticket to view full details and attachments
- Filter tickets by status or date

### Ticket Files

Each ticket can have multiple file attachments:

- Delivery ticket photos
- Bills of lading
- Signed receipts
- Quality documentation

Click **View Files** on any ticket to see all attached documents.

## Mix Log History

SubBase maintains a complete audit trail for all mix log changes:

### Viewing History

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Mix Log">
    Navigate to the pour and select a mix log entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click History Tab">
    Open the **History** tab to view the audit trail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Changes">
    See all changes made to the mix log, including:
    - Who made each change
    - When the change occurred
    - What values were modified (before and after)
    - Nested ticket and attachment changes
  </Step>
</Steps>

The history tab provides full visibility into all modifications for compliance and troubleshooting.

## Exporting Data

Export your concrete commitment data for reporting and analysis:

### Export to Excel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Log Table">
    Go to the pour log table you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Date Range">
    Use the date range filter to select the period you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Click **Export to Excel** in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download File">
    An XLSX file downloads with all log data for the selected period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Exported data includes:
- Pour dates and locations
- Mix codes and designs
- Planned vs. actual quantities
- Variance calculations
- Ticket information

## Tracking Commitment Usage

### Commitment Progress

Monitor how much of your commitment has been used:

- **Total Committed CY** – Cubic yards in the agreement
- **Poured CY** – What's been placed
- **Scheduled CY** – Upcoming pours
- **Remaining CY** – Available for future pours

### Cost Tracking

Track spend against commitment:

- Actual costs per pour
- Comparison to committed pricing
- Any additional charges (pumping, overtime, etc.)

---

## Handling Changes

### Rescheduling Pours

To move a pour to a different date:

1. Open the pour
2. Click **Reschedule**
3. Select new date and time
4. Notify vendor of change

### Adjusting Volumes

If pour requirements change:

1. Open the pour
2. Update the estimated volume
3. Notify vendor if significant change
4. Track actual vs. estimated after pour

### Weather Delays

For weather-related issues:

1. Monitor weather forecasts
2. Communicate early with vendor
3. Reschedule proactively
4. Document weather-related changes
